📚 Literary Consistency Checker (KDSH 2026 Entry)Team: [Your Team Name]Event: Kharagpur Data Science Hackathon (KDSH) 2026📝 Project OverviewThis project was developed for the KDSH 2026 competition. The objective was to detect hallucinations and factual inconsistencies in LLM-generated summaries of classic literature (e.g., The Count of Monte Cristo, In Search of the Castaways).Our solution utilizes a two-pass verification system to cross-reference generated claims against established literary canon, assigning binary consistency labels (0 = Contradiction, 1 = Consistent) supported by natural language rationales.🚀 Key FeaturesAutomated Fact-Checking: Verifies story beats against ground-truth character constraints (e.g., Noirtier's paralysis, Faria's imprisonment dates).Hybrid Scoring System: Combines a heuristic confidence score with semantic analysis to handle "weak" or "unclear" claims.Rationale Generation: Produces human-readable explanations for why a claim is rejected or accepted.🛠️ Methodology1. Data ProcessingWe processed a dataset of story segments involving complex character interactions. The challenge was to distinguish between plausible off-screen events (which are consistent) and impossible plot breaks (contradictions).2. The PipelineOur approach used a distinct multi-stage workflow:Initial Prediction: The model made a binary prediction based on raw text.Confidence Scoring: We calculated a confidence score (0.0 - 1.0) for every claim.Rebalancing Strategy:High Confidence: Accepted the initial prediction.Low/Unclear Confidence: Applied a "Soft Rebalance" to shift borderline cases based on heuristic biases (e.g., penalizing "Long Claim Bias").Rationale Synthesis: Final output includes a narrative explanation (e.g., "Contradicts Faria's 1815 imprisonment...").📊 Results & AnalysisDuring our internal validation phase, we compared our raw score-based predictions against the final narrative-aligned outputs.MetricValueTotal Test Samples60Pipeline Convergence66.7%Primary Rebalance LogicSoft Rebalance & Long Claim Bias correctionNote: The discrepancies between raw scores and final rationales highlight edge cases where the model correctly identified "plausible" events that simple keyword matching would have flagged as errors.📂 Repository StructureBash├── data/
